THE MANIFESTO

WHY WE BUILT DRIPSTER

College hostel rooms and dorms often look like hospital waiting wards: blank eggshell-white cinder blocks, flickering fluorescent lights, and completely devoid of character.

Most poster brands either sell blurry pixelated low-res prints ripped from Google Images on flimsy printer paper, or charge $120 for "gallery art" that no college student can afford.

DRIPSTER was started in a design school dorm room to fix this exact imbalance. We treat wall prints the same way streetwear brands treat heavyweight hoodies: ultra-saturated pigment inks, genuine 300 GSM museum-grade cotton cardstock, curated modern typography, and frames designed to mount with zero damage to college dorm walls.

GOT QUESTIONS?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know about sizing, framing, and college delivery.

Will these ruin my hostel / dorm walls when I move out?

No! Every single order includes a pack of removable damage-free hanging strips. They peel off cleanly without chipping paint or leaving sticky residue so you get 100% of your room deposit back.

What paper quality do you use?

We use heavyweight 300 GSM cotton rag with a smooth satin-matte finish. It has zero annoying glare under desk lamps and feels like luxury art gallery board, not flimsy poster gloss.

Can you deliver straight to my college mail room?

Yes. Just enter your campus address, hall name, and box number at checkout. Our carriers drop directly to university post counters daily.

What does the "Drop" mean? Are they ever restocked?

Pieces marked "THE DROP" or "LIMITED EDITION" are capped at strictly 50 to 120 numbered prints. Once an edition sells out, the vector plates are retired forever.

How does the Custom 1-of-1 order work?

You choose a style in our Custom Order page and enter your custom text or concept. Our design crew drafts a vector mockup in 24 hours. Once you review and approve it, we print and ship within 48 hours.
